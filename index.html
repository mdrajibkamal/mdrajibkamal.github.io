Welcome!

I am Rajib, a PhD Candidate in Finance at NTNU Business School, Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU), Norway. My research interests lie primarily in banking and financial intermediation, corporate finance, sustainable and climate finance, and entrepreneurial finance.

My research examines how climate and environmental policies affect financial institutions, firms, and entrepreneurial activity. My doctoral thesis, Essays on Finance & Banking, studies these issues using empirical settings involving banks, businesses, and climate-related regulation. I have also been a visiting PhD researcher at UQ Business School, University of Queensland, and BI Norwegian Business School.

Before joining NTNU, I completed an MSc in Business Administration (Finance) at Umeå University, Sweden, an MBA in Finance & Banking at Jahangirnagar University, Bangladesh, and a BBA in Agribusiness at Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University, Bangladesh.
