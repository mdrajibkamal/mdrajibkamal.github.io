Short Bio

I am a PhD Candidate in Finance at NTNU Business School, Norwegian University of Science and Technology. My research focuses on banking and financial intermediation, corporate finance, sustainable and climate finance, and entrepreneurial finance.

My doctoral thesis, Essays on Finance and Banking , studies how climate-related regulation affects financial institutions and real economic activity. I have also been a visiting PhD researcher at the University of Queensland and BI Norwegian Business School.

Research interests: Financial Economics; Banking and Financial Intermediation; Corporate Finance; Sustainable and Climate Finance; Entrepreneurial Finance.
